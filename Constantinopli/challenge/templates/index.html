The Fracture of Creation

Before history, there was unity. When scholars sought to formalize reality itself, the world fractured. Constantinople was raised upon the wound between realms. In the aftermath, new paths emerged — some of them exposed as public interfaces rather than hidden passages.

The King Who Watches

The King never ruled from a throne. He ruled from observation. Messages intended for him were not spoken aloud, but instead delivered through a listening path known simply as /thirdphase/listener. Those who understood systems knew this endpoint acted as a webhook, receiving sensitive data sent by the admin.

The Faculty of Science of Tunis

The Faculty was not founded — it manifested. Its scholars mastered machines and symbols, weakening the boundary between worlds. Bug hunters trained within its halls learned how to intercept, observe, and responsibly disclose data flowing through exposed routes.

The Engineers of the Gate

Only those trained to reason formally could approach the portal without losing themselves. They were known as engineers. When important information was obtained, they preserved it using a dedicated storage path in the following form:

/thirdphase/store/<yourUserNameOfTheWebhook>?data=<importantDataToStore>

The Sealing of the Portal

When the last experiment failed, the portal sealed itself. The King did not intervene. Knowledge remained — but access was lost. Only those who controlled the message flow and storage paths could still influence what the system remembered.
