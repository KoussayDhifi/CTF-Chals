ChallengeName:Enectom-Iftar Portal
Difficulty: Medium
----

During Ramadhan, Enet’com deployed Enectom-Iftar, an internal portal promising secure access for staff and students to see what they can eat during Iftar.

While the authentication system was modernized, parts of the old infrastructure were retained to maintain compatibility with legacy routines.

Your goal: access restricted staff-only resources. Observe the portal carefully; the night is long, and patience is rewarded.
