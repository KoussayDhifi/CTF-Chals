## 📖 Challenge Description

Walid Trabelsi runs a shady cryptocurrency exchange where he sells coins to unsuspecting traders. There's a special token called **FLAG** priced at **$1,000.00** — rumored to unlock something extraordinary.

You start with just **$5.00** in your wallet. Can you find a way to acquire the FLAG Token?

The platform looks legitimate. Walid even offers promotional discounts for loyal customers. But as with all things Walid touches — something isn't quite right under the hood.
