**Category:** Web  
**Difficulty:** Medium

**Author:** Koussay DHIFI

## Story

Thousands of clients at InsuranseHorizon have had their insurance claims inexplicably rejected over the past year. Health surgeries denied. Car accident coverage voided. Families left stranded by a company that prides itself on a "98.2% satisfaction rate."

You've been tipped off: the rejection patterns aren't random. Someone — or something — in the admin panel is systematically flagging claims for denial. Internal whistleblowers can't access the system. External auditors have been stonewalled.

Your mission: infiltrate the InsuranseHorizon client portal, escalate your access to the administrative panel, and extract the confidential data buried inside.

The truth is in there. Get it.
